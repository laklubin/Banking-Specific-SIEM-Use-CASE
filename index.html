How to use this library. Treat each entry as a detection specification rather than finished code: confirm the log source is onboarded and parsed, tune thresholds against 30–90 days of your own baseline, and pair every rule with a documented triage and escalation path. Sample logs are synthetic and formatted to resemble real vendor output; account numbers, card numbers, BICs and IP addresses are illustrative.

Mapped to MITRE ATT&CK techniques for coverage reporting. Aligned to the control expectations of PCI DSS, the SWIFT Customer Security Programme, and regulatory cyber-resilience guidance for banks.
